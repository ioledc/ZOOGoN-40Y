# ZooGoN: Gulf of Naples 40-Year Zooplankton Biodiversity Assessment and Standardization

Standardizes taxonomic names in zooplankton datasets from the
LTER-MareChiara station in the Gulf of Naples (1984-2024). This package
is part of the DTO-BioFlow project (Digital Twin Ocean - Biodiversity
Flow Integration) under the EU Horizon Mission to restore ocean and
waters by 2030. It processes 40 years of Mediterranean zooplankton
monitoring data, extracting standardized genus-species information and
converting datasets to Darwin Core Archive format for integration with
EMODnet Biology and the European Digital Twin of the Ocean.
